Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most before people book.

Do you charge a booking fee?+

No. You book and pay the operator directly on their own site — the price you see is the price you pay.

What is meet & greet parking?+

You drive to the terminal, hand your keys to a chauffeur, and they park your car for you. When you land, your car is brought back to the terminal.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong with my booking?+

The operator, since your booking and payment sit with them. Each listing links straight to the operator's own site and contact details.

Is my car safe at the parking compound?+

Every operator we list has to hold accreditation such as Park Mark, along with valid liability cover, before we'll list them.

Will more airports be added?+

Yes — we're starting with Gatwick and adding other UK airports as more operators join.

How far in advance should I book?+

Booking a few weeks ahead usually gets the best price and availability, though most operators also take last-minute bookings.
